Privacy

The app

ServMod works offline. It does not send your parameter backups, printer lists, notes, passwords or any other data anywhere. Everything stays on your phone unless you choose to share a file yourself. The Backup module is the one exception you control: it writes an encrypted copy to a folder you pick, for example in your own Google Drive, and that provider then holds the file under your account.

The Device ID shown in the app is a code derived from your phone. It is used only to create your activation code. The update check downloads one small file and sends no identifier.

This website

The site uses no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics. To see whether anyone visits, our own server counts page views and download clicks: it stores the page, the date and a one-way hash of your IP address and browser that changes every day, so a visitor cannot be followed from one day to the next, and it never stores the IP address itself. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. Cloudflare delivers the site and may process technical request data such as IP address to protect and speed it up.

Requests

If you send a request for a drive, protocol or feature we store what you wrote, the time, and your email address only if you gave one. We use it to decide what to build and to reply if you asked for a reply. We do not add you to any list and we do not share requests.

Waiting list

If you join the waiting list we store your email address, the time you joined and whether you confirmed it, only to send you one email when the first public version of ServMod is released. We ask you to confirm the address first, every message from us has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes the entry. We do not share the list with anyone except the email service that delivers our messages.

Support messages

If you write to support, we keep your name, email address and message to answer you and to help with later questions.

Ordering and licences

When you buy, we store your email address, the Device ID you give us, the activation code we issue and a record of the order (what you bought, when and for how much). We use them only to issue and re-issue your code, to send you receipts and to answer support. Card and PayPal payments are handled by our payment providers, Stripe and PayPal, and we never see your card details; they keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

We keep order records for as long as accounting rules require. You can sign in and delete your account at any time, which removes your login, licences and activation codes, or write to support to ask us to delete the rest.

What we do not keep

We do not store your parameter backups, photos, notes, printer lists, passwords or any other files from the app. They stay on your phone or in the cloud folder you choose yourself. Beyond your email address and the items above, we hold no customer data.
