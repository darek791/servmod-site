Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026. This page covers this website and the version of the app that you download from servmod.com. The version installed from Google Play has its own short page: App privacy (Google Play version). To ask for your data to be deleted, see Delete data.

Website assistant

When the "Ask ServMod" assistant is shown on the site, the text you type is sent to our own server and answered by an AI model that runs on our own computer. It is not sent to any other AI company. We do not store the conversation; we only keep a short-lived counter of requests per visitor to stop abuse. Please do not type personal or confidential data into it. Its answers can be wrong, so always check the manufacturer manual.

The app

ServMod has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It never sends your parameter backups, printer lists, notes, passwords or logs to us. Everything stays on your phone unless you choose to share a file yourself. The app uses the internet only for the following:

  • Updates (version from servmod.com only): it downloads a small version file, and the update file itself when you choose to install it. The request carries the app version number but no personal identifier.
  • Buying and activation: only when you open the purchase or activation screen, the app sends the product, your Device ID and a one-time token to servmod.com. Payment itself is handled by Stripe or PayPal.
  • ServMod Box firmware (licence holders): when you download firmware for the Box, the app sends your licence key to servmod.com so that we can check it.
  • Nameplate, Drive ID, Easy AI and Parts photo search (optional): when you tap "Read with AI" (or use the optional online photo search in Parts) and have accepted the notice, the photo you took goes from your phone straight to the Google Gemini API with your own API key, which is stored only on your phone. It does not pass through us.
  • Local network tools: connections to an address that you type in (for example a PLC, an OBD adapter or the Box) stay on your local network.

Text and barcode recognition run on the phone through Google Play services, which may download its own recognition model; that is done by Google, not by ServMod. The Backup module writes an encrypted copy to a folder you pick, for example in your own Google Drive, and that provider then holds the file under your account.

The Device ID shown in the app is a code derived from your phone. It is sent to us only when you buy or activate, to create your activation code.

This website

The site uses no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics. To see whether anyone visits, our own server counts page views and download clicks: it stores the page, the date and a one-way hash of your IP address and browser that changes every day, so a visitor cannot be followed from one day to the next, and it never stores the IP address itself. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. Cloudflare delivers the site and may process technical request data such as IP address to protect and speed it up.

Requests

If you send a request for a drive, protocol or feature we store what you wrote, the time, and your email address only if you gave one. We use it to decide what to build and to reply if you asked for a reply. We do not add you to any list and we do not share requests.

Waiting list

If you join the waiting list we store your email address, the time you joined and whether you confirmed it, only to tell you when a new version of ServMod is released. We ask you to confirm the address first, every message from us has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes the entry. We do not share the list with anyone except the email service that delivers our messages.

Support messages

If you write to support, we keep your name, email address and message to answer you and to help with later questions.

Ordering and licences

When you buy, we store your email address, the Device ID you give us, the activation code we issue and a record of the order (what you bought, when and for how much). We use them only to issue and re-issue your code, to send you receipts and to answer support. Card and PayPal payments are handled by our payment providers, Stripe and PayPal, and we never see your card details; they keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

We keep order records for as long as accounting rules require. You can sign in and delete your account at any time, which removes your login, licences and activation codes, or write to support to ask us to delete the rest.

What we do not keep

We do not store your parameter backups, photos, notes, printer lists, passwords or any other files from the app. They stay on your phone or in the cloud folder you choose yourself. Beyond your email address and the items above, we hold no customer data.
