Fault codes / Omron MX2 / E08

Omron MX2 fault E08: EEPROM error

Fault while accessing the internal EEPROM, often from noise or excessive temperature. Cannot be reset by RS terminal or STOP/RESET key; power cycle is required.

What to check

  1. Check for strong noise sources nearby and improve EMC measures
  2. Check the heat sink is not clogged
  3. Cycle power; if the error returns, initialise parameters (memory may be corrupt)

Source: 3G3MX2-V2 User's Manual (I666-E1-03, August 2024), page 573. Manufacturer page.
