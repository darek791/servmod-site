Danfoss VLT FC 280 fault codes: meaning and checks

56 fault and warning codes of the Danfoss VLT FC 280, each with what it means and what to check first. Tap a code for the full checklist.

W3No motorNo motor is detected on the drive output.
W4/A4Mains phase lossA supply phase is missing or the supply voltage imbalance is too high. Mains distortion can also cause it.
W7/A7DC overvoltageThe DC-link voltage has exceeded its limit. Mains distortion can also cause it.
W8/A8DC undervoltageThe DC-link voltage has dropped below the low-voltage warning limit. Mains distortion can also cause it.
W9/A9Inverter overloadedThe drive has been carrying more than 100% load for too long.
W10/A10Motor ETR overtemperatureThe electronic thermal model says the motor is too hot after running above 100% load too long.
W11/A11Motor thermistor overtemperatureThe thermistor or its wiring is disconnected, or the motor is overheated.
W12/A12Torque limitTorque has exceeded the motor-mode limit (4-16) or generator-mode limit (4-17).

A16Short circuitThere is a short circuit in the motor or at the motor terminals.
W17/A17Control word timeoutThe drive is no longer receiving communication from the master.
A25Brake resistor short-circuitedThe brake resistor is shorted, so the brake function has been disabled.
W26/A26Brake overloadPower sent to the brake resistor over the last 120 s exceeded the allowed limit.
A27Brake IGBT/chopper short-circuitedThe brake transistor is shorted, so the brake function has been disabled.
A28Brake checkThe brake resistor is not connected or not working.

W40Overload on terminal 27The digital output on terminal 27 is overloaded.
A46Gate drive voltage faultA fault in the gate-drive supply of the power card has been detected.
W47/A4724 V supply lowThe 24 V DC supply may be overloaded.
A49Speed limitMotor speed is below the trip speed low limit set in 1-87.
A50AMA calibration failedA calibration error occurred during the automatic motor adaptation.
A51AMA check Unom and InomThe motor voltage and/or motor current settings are wrong.
A52AMA low InomThe motor current is too low for AMA.
A53AMA big motorThe motor is too large for AMA to run.
A54AMA small motorThe motor is too small for AMA to run.
A55AMA parameter rangeMotor parameter values are outside the acceptable range, so AMA does not run.
A56AMA interruptThe AMA was interrupted.
A57AMA timeoutThe AMA timed out.
A58AMA internalAn internal fault occurred during AMA.

A99Locked rotorThe rotor is blocked.
