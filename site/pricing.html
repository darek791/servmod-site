How a licence works

  1. In the app open Settings, Subscription, and tap Buy (or buy here on this page).
  2. Pay by card or PayPal on the payment provider's secure page.
  3. Go back to the app. It activates by itself in a few seconds.
  4. A receipt arrives by email. If you lose or replace your phone, sign in with that email and move the licence to the new Device ID.

The code is checked inside the app, so ServMod keeps working with no internet connection.

The plans

  • Free: connect, read, monitor, fault history, backup to a file, compare, and all the field tools. No code needed.
  • Pro: a one-time purchase that adds writing parameters, restore, manual control, fault and factory reset, and unlocking writes on the ServMod Box. It stays with the version you own.
  • Subscription: yearly, for modules and drive brands released after you buy, and for updates of parameter lists and profiles.

Prices will be published before ordering opens. Stop always works, in every plan. Prices include all taxes; we are not VAT registered.

What stays the same

  • The app has no ads and no tracking.
  • Update checks only download a small version file.
  • You can try the Free plan before you buy. Read the refunds and licence terms.
