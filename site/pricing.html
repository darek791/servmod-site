Ask before you buy

How a licence will work

  1. You order with your email address and create an account.
  2. The app shows a Device ID. You enter it on your account page.
  3. You receive an activation code by email, and it also stays on your account page.
  4. If you lose or replace your phone, sign in and move the licence to the new Device ID.

The code is checked inside the app, so ServMod keeps working with no internet connection.

What stays the same

  • The app has no ads and no tracking.
  • Update checks only download a small version file.
  • Refund, VAT and licence terms will be final before ordering opens. Drafts are on the refunds and licence pages.
