Modules

What each part of ServMod does today. Status labels are honest: release 0.75 is in testing, and modules marked draft have not been tried on live equipment yet.

ReadyIn field testingDraftPlanned

Inverters

Hitachi WL200, WJ200 and WJ-C1, Allen-Bradley PowerFlex 520 and 525, Danfoss FC 280 and VLT 2800 and Lenze 8400 are tested on real drives. Omron MX2, Mitsubishi FR-D700, Schneider ATV320, Siemens V20, Delta MS300, INVT GD200A, ABB ACS355, Inovance MD500 and Yaskawa GA500 are new and not yet tried on a drive. Hitachi WL200 and Danfoss FC 280 also work over their USB cable.

  • Read all parameters and keep the backup on the phone
  • Restore a backup to the drive
  • Compare a backup with the drive, or the drive with factory settings
  • Edit a single parameter
  • Run the motor manually from the Control screen
  • Faults screen with causes and reset notes from the manual (Hitachi WJ200)

Printers

Honeywell PM45 thermal label printers over a USB-Ethernet adapter.

  • Test the Ethernet port and test-print
  • Cable check: counts link drops, lost replies, delay and speed to show a corroded plug or a damaged cable
  • Keep your own printer list and test one or all
  • PM45 fault guide: error alerts, front panel LEDs, print, network and RFID problems

Tools

The phone's sensors and camera as service instruments.

  • Spirit level, magnet meter for motor and coil fields, ruler and picture measure, height from an angle
  • Tachometer from the camera, magnet, vibration, light sensor or a slow-motion video
  • Slow-motion study: step through a video frame by frame, put two spots on a cylinder and a parcel, and read the delay between them in milliseconds
  • Stroboscope, loupe with light and freeze, colour matcher with RAL names, leak finder using the microphone
  • Sensor explorer: live values of every sensor in the phone

USB Tools

A USB stick analyser and repair kit that works even when Android does not mount the stick.

  • Partitions, raw sectors, speed test and surface scan
  • Fake size check: finds sticks whose real flash is much smaller than the capacity they report
  • Recover tab: undelete on FAT, carve files by signature and find lost partitions (not yet tried on many sticks)
  • Format and repair FAT sticks, save and restore disk images
  • Browse, copy, rename and delete files on the stick
  • Writing is locked behind a confirmation, with an automatic backup before destructive steps

NFC & Barcode

Tags and stickers that open the right thing on the phone.

  • Scan and program NFC tags and barcodes
  • A tag opens a backup with its drive program, a manual or any tool in ServMod
  • Make QR, Code 128, Code 39 and EAN-13 stickers in standard sizes and print them
  • Draft: not yet tried on many phones

Zodiac Printers

Zodiac printer clone files organised on the phone.

  • Library sorted by machine and date, with the jobs inside
  • Label preview and compare of two clones
  • Copy clones to a pendrive for the printer
  • Draft: bench only

ServMod Box and Steppers

Our own ESP32-S3 interface and a stepper motor test bench built on it.

  • RS-485, RS-232, CAN, USB host and Ethernet, reached over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
  • Hitachi WJ200 and WJ-C1 over RS-485 through the Box, Danfoss FC 280 read as fast as with a USB adapter
  • Backup restore runs inside the Box, so a lost phone link does not stop it
  • Ethernet port and cable checks for label printers through the Box
  • Steppers: run, jog, moves, speed and acceleration ladders, resonance sweep, endurance and stress programs, with a simulator
  • Box and Ethernet checked on the bench; CAN and Steppers are still draft; the hardware is a prototype

Thermocontrollers and OBD2

Two newer modules, both drafts.

  • Thermocontrollers: Omron E5CN over RS-485, with present value, setpoint, alarms and parameters
  • OBD2 Scanner: ELM327 adapter over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, trouble codes with descriptions, freeze frame, live data with CSV recording, VIN and readiness monitors
  • Draft: not yet tried on a controller or a car

Backup

Your app data copied to your own cloud folder, encrypted.

  • Choose any folder through the Android picker (Google Drive or another provider)
  • Settings, drive backups with notes, photos, tags, notes, parts and manuals are included; Access passwords and the licence are not
  • Restore on a new phone, adding what is missing or replacing
  • Optional daily copy and a share-by-email copy
  • The key comes from your e-mail address, so the real protection is your own cloud account

PLC, read only

Omron over FINS/UDP (port 9600) and Allen-Bradley Logix or Micro800 over EtherNet/IP (port 44818).

  • Per-machine list of items with live reading every second
  • List of value changes you can copy
  • No write function, by design
  • Draft: tested on a simulator, not yet on a real PLC

Calc, Access, Notes, Parts

Four small tools for the daily routine.

  • Calc: power, cable size, resistor colours, UK wiring colours, Ethernet pinouts
  • Access: encrypted machine passwords
  • Notes: service notes with annotated photos
  • Parts: warehouse search with CSV and XLSX import

ESP32 Loader

Flash ESP32, ESP32-S3 and CYD boards straight from the phone.

  • USB loader for .bin files with offsets
  • MD5 verification and chip check
  • Checked on a real ESP32 CYD board

Condition, Network and My Stuff

Vibration and sound checks with history, Wi-Fi, Ethernet and Bluetooth analysis with ping, port scan and Modbus TCP read, and your own manuals kept offline.

  • In field testing

Service toolbox for Android, made for maintenance engineers.
