App privacy (Google Play version)

Last updated 20 November 2026. This page describes the ServMod app when it is installed from Google Play. The controller of the data is DG, United Kingdom. Contact: support@servmod.com.

In short

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not sell or share your data. Your parameter backups, printer lists, notes, settings, measurements and logs stay on your phone.

What the app sends over the internet

  • ServMod Box firmware (only if you own a licence): when you choose to download firmware for the ServMod Box, the app sends your licence key to servmod.com to check it. We keep a short record of that download (time and the licence it belongs to) to protect the service.
  • Google Gemini (optional): when you tap "Read with AI" in Nameplate, Drive ID or Easy AI and have accepted the notice, the photo you took is sent from your phone to the Google Gemini API with your own API key, which is stored only on your phone. It does not pass through our server. Google processes it under its own terms.
  • Local network: connections to an address that you type in (for example a PLC, an OBD adapter or the ServMod Box) stay on your local network.

This version of the app does not check for updates itself, because Google Play updates it. Text and barcode recognition run on the phone through Google Play services, which may download its own recognition model; that is done by Google, not by ServMod.

Permissions and why

  • Camera: photographing a motor plate or drive label, scanning barcodes, and the measuring tools. Photos are not stored by us.
  • Microphone: the leak finder listens to the sound and analyses it on the phone. Audio is not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Location, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning: finding a nearby ServMod Box or device, and the position used by the Solar tool to calculate the sun's path. The position stays on the phone and is not used for tracking.
  • NFC: reading and writing NFC tags you scan.
  • USB: connecting adapters, drives, printers and the ServMod Box.
  • Files you choose: the Backup module writes an encrypted copy to a folder you pick, for example in your own Google Drive, and that provider then holds the file under your account.

Google Play

Google installs and updates the app and may collect data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that data.

Children

The app is made for professional maintenance technicians and is not directed at children.

Your rights and deleting data

We hold almost no data about users of this version. If you want anything deleted or want to know what we hold, see Delete data or write to support@servmod.com. Under UK GDPR you can also ask for a copy of your data and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. We will update this page when the app changes what it sends.
