About

ServMod is built by one maintenance engineer, Dariusz Grabowski, in the United Kingdom.

I have worked in maintenance for about 30 years: injection moulding, petrochemicals and refineries, robotics, car systems, food production with fast packing lines, and solar panels. Every module in ServMod exists because a job needed it, and I test it on real equipment before I call it ready.

Questions, a drive you would like supported or a fault that is not in the guides are welcome on the support page.

Service toolbox for Android, made for maintenance engineers.
