Supported equipment / Yaskawa GA500

Yaskawa GA500 parameter backup and restore from an Android phone

Back up the settings of a Yaskawa GA500 on your phone, then restore them to a replacement drive in minutes. No laptop, no mobile signal and no software to install on a PC.

How to connect it

  • USB-RS485 adapter on terminals D+ and D- of TB4. Switch S2 to ON on the last drive.
  • ServMod Box RS-485 channel to D+ and D-.

Terminal names differ between production batches. Check the names printed on your unit before you wire anything, and bench-test before you touch a production machine.

What you can do with it in ServMod

  • Read all parameters and save them as a backup file on the phone. Free.
  • Compare a backup with a drive and see every difference. Free.
  • Watch live values and fault history, and look up fault causes. Free.
  • Edit parameters, restore a backup to a new drive, run the motor manually and reset faults. Part of the Pro plan.

Good to know

  • Modbus RTU.
