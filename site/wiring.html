Wiring diagrams

The same diagrams that are inside the app: how to connect each drive, controller or panel to the phone. Tap a diagram to open it full size.

Every drive has one diagram per way of connecting: a USB-RS485 adapter on the phone, the ServMod Box (wireless to the phone), or a direct USB cable where the drive has a USB port. Terminal names differ between production batches, so check the names printed on your unit before you wire anything. Bench-test first, and do not work on a production machine before you have tried it on a bench. Cables and adapters shows what each connection is made of.
