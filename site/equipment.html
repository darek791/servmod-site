Siemens is not supported: it needs the PUT/GET function unlocked in the controller. The PLC module is a draft and has been tested on a simulator only.

Printers

Honeywell PM45 industrial thermal label printers, over Ethernet through a USB-C Ethernet adapter. The Cable check can also test the cable between the phone and any other network device.

Adapters

  • RS-485: a USB-RS485 adapter with an FTDI, CP210x, CH34x or CH343 chip.
  • Ethernet: any USB-C Ethernet adapter. Android handles it natively.
  • One USB-C port: a phone has only one, so for Ethernet and RS-485 together use a powered USB-C hub.
  • CAN: through the ServMod Box. Lenze 8400 is tested this way.

Wiring for each drive is on the connection guides.
