What you get

A personal, non-transferable licence to use ServMod on one Android device at a time. The licence is tied to your account email, not to one phone.

Activation

An activation code is created from your Device ID and signed. The app checks the signature offline. You can move the licence to a new Device ID from your account page if the old phone is lost, broken or replaced.

Updates

Updates are published on this site and the app can check for them from About, Check for updates. Which updates are included in the price will be stated before ordering opens.

Not allowed

Copying, redistributing, sharing activation codes or reverse engineering the app. See also the terms of use.
