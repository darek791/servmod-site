Plans

Free needs no code. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks writing parameters, restore, manual control, fault and factory reset, and keeps working for the version you own. Subscription is yearly and covers modules and drive brands released after you buy, and updates of the parameter lists and profiles. Prices are shown on the pricing page.

What you get

A personal, non-transferable licence to use ServMod on one Android device at a time. The licence belongs to your account email, not to one phone.

Activation

An activation code is created from your Device ID and signed. The app checks the signature offline. You can move the licence to a new Device ID from your account page if the old phone is lost, broken or replaced; a few moves per year are included.

Not allowed

Copying, redistributing, sharing activation codes or reverse engineering the app. See also the terms of use.
