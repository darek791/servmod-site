Connection guides

Wire the drive to the phone with a USB-RS485 adapter. Bench-test before you touch a production machine.

Cables and adapters

What the connections in the guides below are made of. Most phones need the OTG adapter first, then the cable or adapter for the drive.

Omron 3G3MX2 over USB-RS485

Adapter A (D+)
to RS+ on the control terminals
Adapter B (D-)
to RS- on the control terminals
Defaults
Slave 1, 9600 baud, 8-N-1
Termination
Switch on the 120 ohm termination on the last drive

The RJ45 is for the remote operator only, not for ServMod. Changes to C071 to C075 apply after a power cycle.

Hitachi WJ200 and WL200

WL200
USB cable (mini-USB) from the inverter, or a USB-RS485 adapter on the RS-485 terminals
WJ200
USB-RS485 adapter on the RS-485 terminals. The USB cable of the WJ200 does not work with ServMod yet.
Defaults
Slave 1, 9600 baud, 8-N-1

Danfoss VLT FC 280

Adapter A (D+)
to terminal 68 (P)
Adapter B (D-)
to terminal 69 (N)
Defaults
Address 1, 9600 baud, 8 data bits, even parity, 1 stop bit (8-E-1)

Use an isolated adapter if you can, and check the drive's bus parameters (group 8) if it does not answer.

Allen-Bradley PowerFlex 520 and 525

Connection
USB-RS485 adapter on the DSI RJ45, pins 4 (TxRxD+) and 5 (TxRxD-)
Defaults
Node 100, 9600 baud, 8-N-1

The left RJ45 is EtherNet/IP and the right one is DSI.

If the phone does not see the adapter

  • Use a data cable, not a charge-only cable, and an adapter with an FTDI, CP210x, CH34x or CH343 chip.
  • Confirm the USB permission prompt when it appears.
  • Check that slave number, baud rate and format in ServMod match the drive. A mismatch looks like no answer.
  • Swap A and B if the drive still does not answer. Labelling differs between adapters.

Still stuck? Send the drive model and what the screen says through the support form.
